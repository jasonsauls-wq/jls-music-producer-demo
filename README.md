# JLS Music Producer demo

The free demo of JLS Music Producer for Windows 10 and 11. Learners make beats and songs on the computer, and a coach beside the music shows them what to do next.

The demo is the whole program to make music in, with lessons 1 to 4 of Level 1 and the coach. Saving a project to a file, exporting a WAV, certificates, the teacher view and putting loops in the song are in the full program, bought by studios and schools at www.jlsmusic.co.za.
